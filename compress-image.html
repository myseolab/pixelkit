How to use the image compressor

Upload an image, choose the quality and export a smaller image. PNG inputs are exported to WebP to allow lossy compression; JPEG and WebP inputs keep their original format. Images already optimized may get larger rather than smaller.

  1. Choose an image from your device or drop it into the upload box.
  2. Adjust the available settings and click the action button.
  3. Review the result and download your processed image.
