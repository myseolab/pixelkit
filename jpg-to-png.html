How to use the jpg to png converter

Choose a supported image and export it in the target format. JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white. Converting a lossy image to PNG does not recover lost quality.

  1. Choose an image from your device or drop it into the upload box.
  2. Adjust the available settings and click the action button.
  3. Review the result and download your processed image.
