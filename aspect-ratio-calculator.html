How to use the aspect ratio calculator

Set an aspect ratio, choose the known dimension and calculate the matching width or height. Common ratios include 16:9 for landscape video and 1:1 for square graphics.

  1. Enter your aspect ratio and a known pixel dimension.
  2. See the calculated matching dimension.
  3. Use the dimensions in your design.
