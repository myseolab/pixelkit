How to use the image resizer

Upload an image and adjust its dimensions in pixels. Keep the aspect ratio locked to avoid distortion. You can export JPG, PNG or WebP. Resizing an image cannot restore detail that was not present in the original.

  1. Choose an image from your device or drop it into the upload box.
  2. Adjust the available settings and click the action button.
  3. Review the result and download your processed image.
